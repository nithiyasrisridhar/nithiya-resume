Heat Safe

Introduction

Heat Safe is a smart safety application designed to create awareness about heat-related health risks. The system helps users monitor heat conditions and provides safety guidance to reduce the chances of heat stroke and dehydration.

Objective

To provide users with information about heat conditions and preventive measures that improve public safety during high-temperature situations.

Problem Statement

Extreme temperatures can lead to dehydration, heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Many people are unaware of the risks and preventive measures. Heat Safe aims to educate users and improve awareness through an easy-to-use application.

Key Features

🌡 Temperature Monitoring

Displays temperature-related information.

💧 Safety Tips

Provides hydration and heat safety recommendations.

⚠ Alerts

Warns users about extreme heat conditions.

📍 User Friendly Interface

Simple and easy-to-use application.

Technologies Used

HTML
CSS
Android Studio
Java

Project Output

Result

The Heat Safe application provides useful information and safety guidance to users during high-temperature conditions, helping improve awareness and reduce health risks.
